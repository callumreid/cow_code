# the hype film

`cowcode-hype.gif` — the film, as the README displays it.
`cowcode-hype.mp4` — the film WITH the score. the audio is load-bearing.

made in one fever dream with ffmpeg, numpy, and the macOS `say` choir
(Zarvox on narration, Whisper on prophecy, Trinoids on accusations, Fred on
arithmetic, and the Cellos voice singing the outro hymn in the style of Grieg).
the mooo is real — harvested from a live screen recording of Tab being pressed.
big chungus detonates at the 27-second mark as is tradition. the popemobile
orbits the outro cow and briefly becomes its hat. this was not planned. the
machine wanted it.

## regeneration ritual

- `build.sh` — renders the nine segments and concatenates them (ffmpeg only)
- `score.py` — synthesizes the ENTIRE soundtrack from sine waves, noise,
  exponential decay, and belief (numpy only), then places the voices and the
  harvested mooo at frame-locked cues

paths are machine-local to the original barn (`~/screenshots/cowCode*.mov`,
one big_chungus_pop.mp4 of unknown provenance, and seven inspirational pngs
recovered from a chat transcript). without those relics the scripts are
scripture, not software.

no heifers were harmed. mooo responsibly.
